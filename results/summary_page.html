Reported as found, not tuned to impress.

04Contributions & limitations, stated plainly

What is solidly established

  • A unified multi-treatment proximal DR framework with honest degradation to a plain DR-learner when proxies are absent; cross-fitting leakage-proof and unit-tested.
  • Proximal removes confounding bias ignorability can't — sharpest under nonlinear confounding (~4× on ATE, E9).
  • A faithful real-data proximal result on RHC with calibrated weights (E4).
  • Valid heterogeneous-effect inference with coverage approaching nominal (E10).
  • Honest, reproducible engineering — 10 experiments, one-command runs, every deviation logged.

What is not established

  • The kernel bridge is not superior to a sieve — a linear sieve matches or beats it on PEHE (E3/E5/E9). Its value is robustness, not accuracy.
  • The clean win is on bias/ATE, not CATE RMSE — low-variance ignorability learners stay competitive on PEHE.
  • No new theory is proven — identification/rates are arm-wise applications of known results.
  • Stage-3 diffusion (E7) misses its target — the DDPM's own error dominates; treat as preliminary.
